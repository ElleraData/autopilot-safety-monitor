    How alerts are decided

    Each scan is checked against these rules. The highest severity wins, and every rule that fired is listed as a reason. All numbers come from src/config.js.

    stopping distance = v × treaction + v² / (2 × μ × weather × g × brakes), with v in m/s

    • The object ahead is treated as stationary, so relative speed is ignored and time to collision is distance ÷ own speed.
    • Sensor values are generated for each scan from the run seed. They are not integrated from a vehicle model.
    • The vehicle is a generic EV sedan. It does not describe any real vehicle or brand.
